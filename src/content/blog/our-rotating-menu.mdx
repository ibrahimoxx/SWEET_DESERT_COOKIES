Every Monday, we introduce six new cookie flavors. By Sunday, they are gone — ready to make way for the next week's lineup.

## The Philosophy Behind the Rotation

Baking the same thing every day can make you complacent. Our rotating menu keeps our team sharp and our flavors honest. Each week, our bakers develop, test, and refine a new set of recipes from scratch.

That process means every cookie that ends up in our showcase was earned — it beat out every other idea that week.

## How We Develop New Flavors

Our flavor development starts early in the week and locks in by Thursday for the following Monday debut. Our bakers test dozens of variations before settling on the six that make the cut.

We take inspiration from seasons, ingredients at their peak, collaborations with our community, and pure curiosity.

## The Classics Are Always Here

Our rotating menu does not replace our classics — it complements them. The Pink Sugar Cookie, the Milk Chocolate Chip, and our other permanent flavors are always available.

The classics are classics for a reason. The weekly rotation is what keeps things exciting.

## Stay Updated

Follow us on social media or download the Sweet Desert App to get notified every Monday when the new menu drops. Some flavors sell out fast — early is better.
