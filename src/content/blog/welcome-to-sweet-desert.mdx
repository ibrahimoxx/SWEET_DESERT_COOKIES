We started Sweet Desert with one idea: desserts are better when shared.

## Our Story

Every Sweet Desert location is built around an open kitchen — a place where you can watch our bakers work in real time, filling each box with care. From the moment you walk in, you are part of the experience.

The name says it all. Sweet, because that is what we do. Desert, because great things grow in unexpected places — and we believe the best desserts do too.

## What Makes Us Different

We rotate our menu every week. Six new flavors, crafted and tested by our team, arrive every Monday. Classic flavors — the ones you already love — are always there too.

We believe variety is what makes dessert exciting. A rotating menu means there is always a reason to come back.

## Built for Community

Sweet Desert was built for people who appreciate the details — the perfect texture, the right amount of frosting, the experience of opening a fresh box. We are here to make every occasion a little sweeter.

Whether you are celebrating a birthday, treating your team, or just having a Tuesday, we are glad you are here.

Welcome to Sweet Desert.
