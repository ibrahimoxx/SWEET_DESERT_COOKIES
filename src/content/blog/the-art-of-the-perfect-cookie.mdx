After thousands of batches, our bakers have developed a few firm beliefs about what makes a cookie worth eating.

## It Starts with the Butter

Room temperature butter is not optional — it is essential. Creaming cold butter with sugar produces a crumbly, dense result. Creaming soft butter creates the emulsion that gives cookies their lift, their chew, and their structure.

Every Sweet Desert batch starts with butter that has been resting at room temperature for at least two hours.

## The Chill Matters

Most of our cookie doughs are refrigerated overnight before baking. The cold rest gives the flour time to hydrate fully, developing gluten and concentrating flavor.

The result is a cookie with more complexity, better texture, and a thicker profile that holds its shape in the oven.

## Baked Fresh Throughout the Day

We do not bake in advance and hope for the best. Every cookie at Sweet Desert is baked throughout the day, so what you get is as close to fresh out of the oven as a boxed cookie can be.

The open kitchen concept is not just for show — it is how we ensure freshness.

## Frosting Is Not an Afterthought

A cookie is only as good as its frosting. We develop our frosting recipes with the same care as the cookie base — testing ratios, temperatures, and textures until they complement rather than overpower.

We believe frosting should enhance the cookie, not hide it.

## Why This All Matters

A perfect cookie is a small thing. But small things done well add up to an experience worth coming back for.

That is what we are after at Sweet Desert — not just a good cookie, but the best cookie you have had this week.
